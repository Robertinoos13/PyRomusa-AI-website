Mission and Focus


Discover why PyRomusa AI exists and what it focuses on.


The beginning of history...

Some time ago, the creator of PyRomusa AI wanted to build a modern chatbot, similar to ChatGPT, that would run entirely offline on his own hardware. However, he faced a challenge: he couldn't find the ideal resources to learn how to do it, making the learning process quite difficult and leading him to put the project on hold for a while.


A few months later, the creator reconsidered this learning plan; unable to accept the lack of perfect learning resources to achieve his goal, he came up with the idea of ​​creating the PyRomusa AI framework.


Yes, at the time, the creator knew absolutely nothing about how ChatGPT worked under the hood, so he devised a custom architecture optimized to utilize only a few functions, without unnecessary complexity.


FUN FACT: Initially, PyRomusa AI was called Muri AI, but its name was changed for better and more unique branding.
